## Prompt

Create a polished production app icon for OpenConnect Multi, a desktop VPN profile manager. Single centered original geometric symbol: two interlocking rounded open links forming a subtle O and C, suggesting a secure connection. Bold white and pale cyan mark on a rich cobalt blue rounded-square tile, very subtle blue gradient, clean precise vector-like edges, balanced negative space, high legibility at 24 pixels. Modern understated premium software identity. No text, no letters drawn as typography, no padlock, no globe, no shield, no paper plane, no mockup, no extra objects. Square 1024x1024 asset, tile fills canvas, no external margin, flat front-on view.
